Getting started

When you open the game you have two choices.

  1. Sign in or create an account. Use an email and a password, or Continue with Apple or Continue with Google. The first time with email, tap Create my login.
  2. Play the computer as a guest. No account needed.

Your login

  • One login per person. Use the same one on any phone or computer and your name, points and friends come back.
  • Your email is never shown to other players.
  • Your name is chosen once you are in, under Settings. It is unique and permanent.
  • Forgot your password? Type your email and tap Forgot your password?. A link arrives by email; check your spam folder too.

2. How do you play?

  • Against the computer: the computer takes the other seats.
  • Same phone, friends: up to 4 people share one phone. Each picks a name and a picture. Pass the phone when it is the next player's turn.

3. Players

2, 3 or 4.

4. Points to play

Friendly costs nothing. Any other amount goes into a pot; see Points.

Signed-in players can also set Time per turn: Off, 10, 15 or 30 seconds.

On the same phone, each colour has its own name box, four animal pictures and a Photo button to use a picture from the phone.

Two players cannot use the same name.

Playing a turn

  1. Your kër lights up and your name shows under the board.
  2. Tap Roll. The dice show large in the centre of the board for a moment, so everyone round the phone can see.
  3. Pieces that can move glow. Tap one to move it.
  4. With two dice, tap a die first, then a piece. You can play both dice on the same piece.

If no move is possible, the game says so and the turn passes.

The turn timer

When a timer is set, the seconds left show on the current player's kër on every phone.

  • Time runs out before you roll: the turn passes. The game never rolls for you.
  • Time runs out after you roll: the game moves a piece for you.

What the kër shows

Each player's kër shows their name, picture and points. Pieces you catch sit in your kër. The word follows the country in your profile: kër for Senegal, home in most other countries, maison in France.

The rules, drawn

These drawings follow Teranga Classic. The Rules button in the game always shows the rules of the game you are playing.

Winning

The first player with all four pieces home wins. With 3 or 4 players the game carries on to decide 2nd, 3rd and 4th.

West African, with 2 dice

Play each die on any piece, or both on the same piece. A double 6 gives another roll.

Friendly

Caught pieces go back to their own yard. Pieces on star squares cannot be caught.

Fair dice

The dice are plain chance, the same for everyone. Points and entries never change the odds.

Play online

Start a private game

  1. Tap Play online, then Play with friends.
  2. Choose the game, players, points and when the game starts:
    • When I tap Start, or
    • By itself when full.
  3. Tap Create game. You get a six-character code.
  4. Tap Invite players to share the link, or tap Invite next to a friend.
  5. Tap Start game. Empty seats are played by the computer.

Only people with your link, code or invitation can join. Every new game gets a new code.

Join a game

Open the link you were sent, or type the code under Join with a code.

Joining a game already started

The host sees a request and taps Accept or Decline. If accepted, the new player takes over a computer seat and pays the entry.

When someone goes quiet

"away" shows next to their name and the game plays safely for them until they come back.

Leaving

Leave game during a game means you lose that game for good.

After the game

The room closes 12 seconds after the end. Tap Play again before then and everyone moves to a new room together.

If your phone drops the connection, the game brings you back to your seat with your pieces as they were.

Friends and invitations

  • Your friend code is under Play online → Play with friends, at the bottom. Tap Share to send it.
  • To add a friend, type their friend code or their name and tap Add. You can also tap + Friend next to someone you play with.
  • In your private game, tap Invite next to a friend. They see a bar with Join and Not now.
  • Tap Remove to take someone off your list.

Chat, block and report

During an online game, a row of emojis sits under the board.

  1. Tap → All to choose who sees it: everyone or one player. A message to one player shows only on their phone and yours.
  2. Tap an emoji. It flies from your kër to theirs, with its own sound. The list has the Senegal flag and the flag of the country in your profile.
  3. Tap 💬 for ready-made phrases, or type up to 40 characters and tap Send.

Rude words are hidden automatically.

  • Block hides that player's messages, emojis and invitations on your device. Unblock any time in Settings.
  • Report sends us a report and blocks the player. Reported players can be removed from the game.

Be respectful. Insults and harassment are not allowed. See the rules of conduct.

Results and statistics

At the end you see the final places and the points each player won. Then choose Play again, Scores or New game.

Confetti is for the winner only, on the winner's own phone.

Tap Scores to see:

  • My statistics: win rate, games played, won and lost, current and best win streak, pieces captured and captures per game.
  • Leaderboard: points, wins, games, catches and best run.
  • Recent games.

Signed in, your statistics are saved with your login. Guests are not on the leaderboard.

Delete my account

Settings → Your account → Delete my account, then confirm. Your name, picture, points, results and friends are erased for good. If you used Apple, the link with your Apple ID is removed too.

Played before with your phone number?

Create your login once. On the phone you already used, your points follow by themselves. On another phone, open Played before with your phone number? under your account and type the number once. The number is used only for this and is never shown to anyone.

Problems and questions

My points are missing on a new phone

Sign in the same way as before: the same email and password, or the same Apple or Google account. Guest points stay on the device where they were won, and only until the game is closed.

"This email already has a login"

Sign in the way you did the first time, for example with email and password instead of Google.

I did not get the password email

Check your spam folder. Wait a few minutes, then tap Forgot your password? again.

"No game found with this code"

Check the six characters. A finished game's room closes after 12 seconds, and every new game has a new code. Ask the host for the new link.

"This game is full" or "already started"

Ask the host to accept you. You will take over a computer seat if there is one.

There is no sound

Check the volume and the silent switch, then make sure Sound is On.

The game says it is updating

New versions install by themselves. If a message asks you to, close the game completely and open it again.

Someone is being rude

Tap 💬 under the board, then Block or Report beside their name.

Can I buy points or cash them out?

No. Points are for fun only and have no cash value.

Questions fréquentes

Comment commencer ? Ouvrez le jeu et touchez Se connecter ou créer un compte. Utilisez votre e-mail et votre mot de passe (touchez Créer mon compte la première fois), ou Continuer avec Apple ou Continuer avec Google. Vous choisissez ensuite votre nom et votre image dans Réglages. Vous pouvez aussi toucher Jouer contre l'ordinateur en invité pour jouer sans compte.

Comment jouer avec des personnes ailleurs ? Touchez Jouer avec des amis, créez une partie et partagez le lien d'invitation ou le code à six caractères. Il faut être connecté pour jouer en ligne.

Mes points ont disparu sur un nouveau téléphone. Connectez-vous de la même façon qu'avant : le même e-mail et le même mot de passe, ou le même compte Google ou Apple. Votre nom, vos points et vos amis reviennent. Les points gagnés en invité restent sur l'appareil où ils ont été gagnés.

J'ai oublié mon mot de passe. Tapez votre e-mail dans Réglages et touchez Mot de passe oublié ?. Un lien pour en choisir un nouveau vous est envoyé. Regardez aussi dans vos indésirables.

Comment changer mon nom, mon image ou mon pays ? Ouvrez Réglages. Votre nom, votre image et votre pays se trouvent sous votre compte une fois connecté.

Je me connectais avec mon numéro de téléphone. Créez votre compte une fois avec un e-mail et un mot de passe. Sur le téléphone que vous utilisiez déjà, vos points suivent tout seuls. Sur un autre téléphone, ouvrez Vous jouiez avant avec votre numéro ? sous votre compte et tapez le numéro une fois.

Il n'y a pas de son. Vérifiez le volume et le bouton silencieux, puis touchez le bouton du son dans le jeu.

Le jeu annonce une mise à jour. Touchez la barre jaune, ou fermez le jeu et rouvrez-le.

Quelqu'un est impoli. Pendant une partie en ligne, touchez la bulle, puis Bloquer ou Signaler à côté du nom du joueur.

Comment supprimer mon compte ? Ouvrez Réglages, allez à Votre compte et touchez Supprimer mon compte. Si vous avez utilisé Apple, le lien avec votre identifiant Apple est aussi retiré.

Peut-on acheter des points ou les convertir en argent ? Non. Les points servent uniquement à jouer et n'ont aucune valeur en argent.

Contatti

Scrivi a support@niororesources.com. Indica il telefono o il computer che usi e cosa è successo. Non inviarci mai la tua password per nessun servizio; non la chiediamo.

Domande frequenti

Come comincio? Apri il gioco e tocca Accedi o crea un account. Usa la tua e-mail e la password (tocca Crea il mio account la prima volta), oppure Continua con Apple o Continua con Google. Scegli il tuo nome e la tua immagine dopo, in Impostazioni. Puoi anche toccare Gioca contro il computer come ospite per giocare senza un account.

Come gioco con persone in altri posti? Tocca Gioca con gli amici, crea una partita e condividi il link d'invito o il codice di sei caratteri. Devi aver effettuato l'accesso per giocare online.

I miei punti non ci sono su un nuovo telefono. Accedi nello stesso modo di prima: la stessa e-mail e la stessa password, o lo stesso account Google o Apple. Il tuo nome, i punti e gli amici tornano. I punti guadagnati come ospite restano sul dispositivo dove sono stati guadagnati.

Ho dimenticato la password. Scrivi la tua e-mail in Impostazioni e tocca Password dimenticata?. Ti viene inviato un link per sceglierne una nuova. Controlla anche la cartella dello spam.

Come cambio nome, immagine o paese? Apri Impostazioni. Nome, immagine e paese si trovano sotto il tuo account una volta effettuato l'accesso.

Accedevo con il mio numero di telefono. Crea il tuo account una volta con un'e-mail e una password. Sul telefono che usavi già, i tuoi punti ti seguono da soli. Su un altro telefono, apri Giocavi prima con il tuo numero? sotto il tuo account e scrivi il numero una volta.

Non c'è audio. Controlla il volume e l'interruttore del silenzio, poi tocca il pulsante dell'audio nel gioco.

Il gioco dice che c'è un aggiornamento. Tocca la barra gialla, oppure chiudi il gioco e riaprilo.

Qualcuno è maleducato. Durante una partita online, tocca il fumetto, poi Blocca o Segnala accanto al nome del giocatore.

Come elimino il mio account? Apri Impostazioni, vai a Il tuo account e tocca Elimina il mio account. Se hai usato Apple, viene rimosso anche il collegamento con il tuo ID Apple.

Posso comprare o convertire in denaro i punti? No. I punti servono solo per divertirsi e non hanno valore in denaro.
